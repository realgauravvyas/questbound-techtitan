# Artwork

public/realm.webp is original AI-generated art created for Questbound using the built-in image-generation tool. It is saved in the project and optimized from a 1536 x 1024 raster image.

Prompt: A polished wide painterly pixel-art fantasy landscape for a Life RPG: a lone tiny traveler in an orange cloak with a backpack stands on a grassy cliff at right foreground, overlooking a floating ancient stone sanctuary, waterfalls, pine forests and layered teal mountains at warm sunrise. Tiny fireflies; crisp premium indie RPG detail; darker quiet left side for text; saturated forest teal and midnight blue with golden light. Exactly one image, no text, no UI, no watermark.

Interface symbols are from lucide-react. The compass favicon is simple functional SVG geometry. No stock image hotlinks, paid assets, or copied game sprites are required.
